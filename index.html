The Voice House

Every voice in this house was made on one PC (an RTX 4080 SUPER) with VoiceStudio, a free, open-source app that runs speech models on your own computer. Each room shows one thing it can do, with the real sound it made, an animation that follows the sound, and a care tag with exactly what went in, what came back, the call that made it, and what it took: seconds of sound, seconds of GPU work and watt-hours.

The house's own voices (Nell, Pip, Professor Hoot, Tilly, Mabel and Otto) were designed from a few words each. No real person's voice is cloned anywhere in the house. Every take carries VoiceStudio's inaudible AI watermark; the attic shows it.
